FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything business owners ask before starting a project — answered honestly.

How long does it take to build my website?
+
Most standard business websites go live within 7–14 business days from when we start. Ecommerce stores and complex custom applications typically take 3–6 weeks. I provide a clear timeline during your free consultation, so you'll always know when to expect your website.
Will my website look good on mobile phones?
+
Absolutely — every BrainLab website is built mobile-first and tested on 15+ real devices before launch. Since 70%+ of your customers will visit on a smartphone, this is non-negotiable for us. Your website will look and work flawlessly on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops.
Do you help with domain name and web hosting?
+
Yes, completely. I handle domain registration, hosting setup, SSL security certificate, and deployment from start to finish. You don't need any technical knowledge — just provide your business name and I'll take care of the rest. Ongoing hosting management is also available.
Will my website appear on Google (SEO)?
+
Every website I build includes a strong SEO foundation: fast loading speed, clean code, proper heading structure, meta tags, schema markup, XML sitemap, and Google Analytics setup. This gets you ranking-ready from day one. For ongoing local SEO campaigns to dominate your area, I offer additional monthly packages.
Can I update my website content myself after launch?
+
Yes! I can build your website with an easy CMS (content management system) so you can update text, images, prices, menus, or blog posts yourself — no coding needed. I also provide full training and walkthrough videos so you feel confident managing your own site.
What happens after my website goes live?
+
I don't disappear after launch. All packages include post-launch support. I offer ongoing maintenance plans covering security updates, backups, performance monitoring, content changes, and priority support via WhatsApp. I treat every client as a long-term partner, not a one-time transaction.
How much does a professional website cost?
+
BrainLab packages start at ₹14,999 for a professional business website. The exact cost depends on the number of pages, features, and complexity required. EMI payment arrangements are available. I recommend booking a free consultation first — I'll recommend the right option for your specific needs and budget, without any sales pressure.
